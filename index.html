關於
Aira About
Aira

我是曾歆晴，一名夢想成為演員的表演者。

我喜歡演戲，是因為希望能在短暫的人生裡， 體驗不同的人生活法。

透過與角色的共振， 我試著理解並傳達角色的情感。

我喜歡有故事感的角色，也享受在閱讀劇本時 逐漸與角色產生連結。

希望我的表演能讓觀眾感受到療癒， 或得到一些啟發。

I am Aira Tseng, a young performer who dreams of becoming an actress.

I love acting because it allows me to experience different ways of living within one lifetime. By connecting with a character, I try to understand and communicate their emotions.

I am drawn to characters with a strong sense of story, and I enjoy gradually building a connection with a character while reading a script.

I hope my performances can bring audiences a sense of healing, inspiration, or simply a moment worth remembering.

我的
表演理念 Acting
Philosophy

我是曾歆晴，一名夢想成為演員的表演者。

我喜歡演戲，是因為希望能在短暫的人生裡， 體驗不同的人生活法。

透過與角色的共振， 我試著理解並傳達角色的情感。

我喜歡有故事感的角色，也享受在閱讀劇本時 逐漸與角色產生連結。

希望我的表演能讓觀眾感受到療癒， 或得到一些啟發。

I am Aira Tseng, a performer who hopes to become an actress and explore the many different lives that stories can offer.

I love acting because every character gives me an opportunity to experience another perspective and another way of living.

When reading a script, I enjoy discovering the small details that gradually build a connection between myself and the character.

Through acting, I hope to create moments that bring audiences comfort, inspiration, or a new way of seeing the world.
